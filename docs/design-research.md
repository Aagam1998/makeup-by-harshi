# Makeup artist website research

Reviewed 24 October 2026. These observations are design references, not evidence of conversion performance. No competitor photographs, testimonials, prices, credentials, or text have been imported.

## Hannah Monk Hair & Makeup
https://www.hannahmakeup.co.uk/

Visually inspected the photo-led masthead. The homepage introduces the artist, offers a direct availability enquiry, and separates bridal, lessons, and special occasions. Keep Harshi's photography prominent and make both the artist and enquiry route easy to find. A real portrait will improve this further once supplied.

## Tejas Shah Makeup
https://www.tejasshahmakeup.com/

Reviewed the homepage's service, portfolio, booking, and education paths. Make the path from an interesting photograph to an enquiry more direct. Implemented selected-look references in the contact form, with explicit sample-photography disclosure.

## Namrata Soni Academy
https://www.namratasoniacademy.com/

Reviewed its dedicated education navigation, artist introduction, gallery, student work, contact, and FAQ access. Keep Harshi's education area distinct from bridal services. Course dates, student results, qualifications, and course availability must wait for confirmation.

## Changes implemented

- Gallery viewer links carry the selected look and service into contact enquiries.
- The enquiry form displays an optional, changeable inspiration reference.
- Prepared messages identify stock imagery as a sample reference, not Harshi's work.
- Homepage uses clearer lookbook wording and four native, keyboard-accessible booking FAQs.
- FAQ content is stored as typed records for future CMS integration.

## Highest-impact next content additions

Approved Harshi portfolio photographs, her own portrait, a verified WhatsApp number, and authentic testimonials when supplied. These will provide more value than additional decorative effects.
